Employee Referral Program

Know someone great? Refer them.

Employee referrals are one of the most effective ways we recruit top-notch employees.

Eligible Jobs & Rewards

  • Paratransit Driver $200
  • Direct Care $100

If an employee refers a person who is hired and remains on the job for at least three months, the employee may receive a reward of up to $200, less applicable taxes. The employee must still be employed by Caring Hands when the reward payment is made.

Who Can Make a Referral?

Employees are eligible to make referrals if they are not involved in the hiring process for the referred candidate. Human Resources staff members are not eligible.

Who Can Be Referred?

  • Not currently employed by Caring Hands in any capacity.
  • Not previously referred by someone else.
  • Not previously applied for a position at Caring Hands.

Make a Referral

You'll receive an email confirmation to forward to your candidate. They'll use it to apply with your unique referral code.

How Referral Rewards Work

  • The referral was submitted before the candidate was offered an interview, or the candidate indicated the referral on their application.
  • The referred person is hired and remains employed for three months.
  • The referring employee remains employed by Caring Hands when payment is made.

The referral reward is a taxable additional payment included in the employee's regular paycheck, made soon after the referred employee reaches three months of employment.
